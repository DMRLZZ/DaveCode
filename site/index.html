Step-by-step setup ↗

Good questions.

Is DaveCode free?

Yes. DaveCode is open source, with no DaveCode subscription or credits to buy. The AI tools you connect may charge for their own usage.

Can I bring the AI tools I already use?

That’s the idea. The preview includes connections for Claude, Codex and Gemini. What you can use depends on your provider account and plan. See the current connections and preview limitations before setting up.

Do I need to know how to code?

You can describe your idea in everyday language. Today’s preview still needs some technical setup. The upcoming desktop app is being built to make that first step easier.

Where does my work live?

Your project stays on your computer. The AI tool you choose receives the prompts and project context it needs to help you. You decide which provider to connect.
