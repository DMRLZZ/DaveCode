Questions

Is DaveCode free?

Yes. DaveCode is open source under MIT. You use your own agent subscriptions or API keys; DaveCode charges nothing on top.

Why do I need a DaveCode account?

The account identifies you across your machines, in the terminal and the desktop app. It holds your email and sign-in method only. Your agent logins, keys and code stay on your machine.

Which agents work today?

Claude Code, Codex CLI and Antigravity, plus Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini and OpenAI-compatible API keys.

Where does my work live?

On your machine: a local SQLite database, an encrypted keyring and the .davecode/ folder in your project. Only the agent you route to receives your prompt.
