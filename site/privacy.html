Privacy

This website does not use analytics scripts, tracking cookies or signup forms. Cloudflare, which hosts it, processes the request data needed to deliver and protect it.

DaveCode accounts

Signing in to DaveCode stores your email address, your name if your provider shares it, and how you sign in: a Google or GitHub account id, or a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password. Sessions are signed tokens; refresh tokens are stored only as hashes. We send email only to confirm your address.

The application

DaveCode runs on your machine. Your code, prompts, agent logins and API keys stay there, except what you send to the AI provider you choose; that provider's terms and privacy policy apply.

The source is on GitHub. To delete your account, open an issue there or write to the repository owner.
