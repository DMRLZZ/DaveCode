Privacy

This landing page does not collect signups, use analytics scripts or set tracking cookies. Hosting providers can process request metadata needed to deliver and protect the site.

The local application

DaveCode runs a gateway on your machine. Connecting an AI provider sends the prompts and context required for your requests to that provider. Its terms and privacy policy apply.

Optional Google and GitHub sign-in identifies users allowed by the workspace owner. Login uses the provider's official authorization flow. Sessions use a local HttpOnly cookie. Model credentials and app sign-in are separate.

The source and implementation are available on GitHub. Review your deployment configuration before exposing a local gateway to other people.
